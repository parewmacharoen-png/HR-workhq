# WorkHQ Constitution V1

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective:** 2026-06-29

Canonical product principles for WorkHQ v1 Daily Company Operating System.

## Rules

1. **Single work-day truth** — All attendance/leave/off-day operational views resolve through `WorkDayService`.
2. **State before menu** — Employee Telegram home is state-first (Today screen), not a long static menu.
3. **Role-appropriate surfaces** — Employees see task actions; managers see operations, risk, and approvals.
4. **No silent payroll rewrite** — Shift/attendance corrections flag `needsRecalculation`; preview is not final.
5. **Approved-only payroll impact** — Pending/rejected OT never counts in preview.
6. **Monthly off ≠ leave** — Separate entity and workflows.
7. **Bangkok time domain** — Work dates and shift windows use Asia/Bangkok.
8. **Scope by role** — Big Leader/team leaders see scoped company data only.
9. **Exception visibility** — Missing punch, break, OT pending surface in Exception Center.
10. **Minimal employee v1 menu** — No KPI/AI/Training/Competency/Succession on employee main Telegram menu.
11. **Operations hub** — Command Center aggregates work-day widgets + workforce risk.
12. **Forecast before crisis** — Workforce risk projects staffing gaps from work-day availability.
13. **Actionable recommendations** — Risk engine returns recommended responses (OT, standby, reschedule off, alert owner).
14. **Predictive Operations** — Morning brief and ops surfaces include workforce risk signals derived from `WorkDayService` + staffing rules, enabling proactive staffing decisions before customer impact.
